Four ways to get exactly the material or product you need.

Custom Holographic Pattern Design

Send the pattern you have in mind. Our designers separate the effect layers, prepare the paths and make a file suitable for UV lithography, then produce a demo for you to approve.

Pattern Combination & Reserved Areas

Start from ready patterns: arrange them by area, join different background patterns, and reserve space for your main artwork or text. Reserved areas are a design arrangement, not holes cut in the paper.

Packaging boxes, cards, mini cards, postcards and other paper goods made with our materials.

Custom holographic pattern design

Your artwork, turned into a holographic pattern

In this example a single drawing is built from several light effects: fine radial lines on the dial, a brushed texture on the strap, smooth mirror areas and rainbow rings on the bezel. Our designers separate your artwork into effect layers like these and prepare the file for UV lithography.

You approve a demo before anything goes into production. The real effect changes with light and viewing angle.
