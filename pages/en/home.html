FAQ

Common questions

How do I choose a material?

Tell us what you are making, how it will be printed and the look you want. You do not need to know the specification; we will suggest suitable materials.

How do I get a sample?

Use “Request a Sample” on any product or pattern page, or the contact form. The product or pattern number is filled in for you.

Can I have my own holographic pattern?

Yes. Send your artwork. Our designers separate the effect layers and prepare a production file, then make a demo for you to approve. See Customisation.

How does the design fee work?

Custom holographic pattern design and demo is charged at {{design_fee}}. When you place the final order, the design fee you paid is deducted from the cost of the finished goods. Exact scope is confirmed in your quotation.

What are the MOQ, price and lead time?

They depend on material, size and quantity, so we quote each order. Send an inquiry and we will reply with figures for your job.
