Silver paperboard for folding cartons is made in two main ways: by transferring a metallized layer onto the board, or by laminating a metallized PET film to it. On a shelf they can look almost the same. In the press room, on the die-cutter and at the recycling plant they behave differently.

How each board is made

Transfer metallized paperboard

A carrier film is coated with a release layer and metallized with a very thin layer of aluminium. The metallized side is glued to the board, and once the adhesive has set the carrier film is peeled away and reused. What stays on the board is the aluminium layer and a thin coating. No plastic film is left in the finished board.

PET laminated (film laminated) paperboard

A metallized PET film is laminated permanently to the board with adhesive. The film stays on the board and forms its top surface. The film can be plain silver, matte, or carry a holographic effect.

Printing

Both surfaces are non-absorbent, so ink cannot dry by soaking into the paper. Printers normally use inks designed for non-absorbent substrates and a press with suitable drying or curing. Always run a print test on the exact board before a production order, and ask your supplier for the recommended ink type and surface tension.

Converting

Transfer metallized board die-cuts and creases very much like the uncoated base board. Laminated board is stiffer at the surface. It usually needs a slightly different crease depth and channel width, and sharp tools, to keep the film from cracking along the fold. Hot foil stamping and embossing work on both.

Sustainability

If your customer asks for plastic-free or easily recyclable packaging, transfer metallized board is usually the first choice, because the carrier film is removed during production. Recycling rules differ between countries, so confirm the claim you plan to make with your own market's recycling guidance.

Cost

Price depends on board grade, weight, metallizing finish and order quantity. As a rule, transfer metallized board can be competitive at volume because the carrier film is reused. Laminated board carries the cost of the film that stays in the product. Ask for quotes on both for your exact specification.

Quick decision guide

  • Need a plastic-free carton with a bright silver look: choose transfer metallized.
  • Need maximum scuff resistance, a deep mirror or a strong holographic effect: choose PET laminated.
  • Not sure: request samples of both and run them through your own press and die-cutter.
