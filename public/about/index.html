About us

Zhejiang Tianqi Packaging Materials Co., Ltd. specialises in gold and silver paperboard and other packaging papers. Our materials are used for cosmetics, gifts, tea, tobacco, liquor, pharmaceutical and daily-goods packaging.

Zhejiang, China

Our factory

From coating to slitting, production runs in our own plant: laser embossing, coating, vacuum metallising, cast coating, high-speed laminating, rewinding and slitting.

UV lithography

Holographic patterns made in house

Our holographic patterns are produced in our own factory, using UV lithography. The lithography system works in a yellow-light cleanroom, and the patterned material then runs on our coating and UV curing lines.

Innovation

Research and development

Our technology centre works on greener, more functional and higher-performance packaging papers. The items below are development results; ask us whether a grade is available for your order.

Developed grades

  • Eco transfer bright silver board
  • Waterproof, moisture-resistant silver board
  • Grey-back plain holographic laminated silver board
  • Waterproof, flame-retardant kraft board
  • Water-based coated gold and silver board
  • Anti-static, anti-counterfeit gold and silver board for digital printing

Technologies
