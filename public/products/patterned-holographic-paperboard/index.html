Patterned Holographic Paperboard

Patterned Holographic Paperboard

Holographic paperboard with a ready-made pattern. Choose from 200+ patterns in the library or start a custom design.

ProcessUV lithography. Two routes are used: the pattern is made on PET film and then laminated to board, or PET film and board are laminated first and the surface is UV-patterned. The route for each order is confirmed with you.
Visual effectPatterned light effects that change with viewing angle.

Weight, size, supply form, MOQ and lead time are quoted on request.
