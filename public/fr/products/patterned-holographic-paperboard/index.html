Patterned Holographic Paperboard

Patterned Holographic Paperboard

For folding cartons, rigid boxes, cards and labels

Holographic paperboard with a ready-made pattern. Choose from 200+ patterns in the library or start a custom design.

ProcessLithography. Two routes are used: the pattern is made on PET film and then laminated to board, or PET film and board are laminated first and the pattern is made on the surface. The route for each order is confirmed with you.
Visual effectPatterned light effects that change with viewing angle.
RegistrationPatterns can be placed in register with your print.
Anti-counterfeitingHigh-precision lithography can add micro-text and security codes to the pattern.
ConvertingGood folding and die-cutting performance; suitable for hot stamping and lamination.

Weight, size, supply form, MOQ and lead time are quoted on request.
